Ann Taylor's loyalty program is free to join and earns on every qualifying purchase, online or in stores, at 2 points per $1. Every 500 points converts to a $5 reward certificate.

- **The Ann Taylor credit card doubles the earn rate to 5 points per $1** and adds 15% off every first Tuesday of the month, plus free shipping on online orders over $75 (versus the standard $150 threshold). It's a store card decision, not a coupon decision — the rewards don't offset interest if you carry a balance, so it only makes sense if you pay in full.

Note what doesn't earn points: gift cards, taxes, shipping, and the value of coupons themselves. If you're buying a gift card to maximize rewards, that purchase earns nothing.
